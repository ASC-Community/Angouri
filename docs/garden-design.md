# The moonlit garden

The reward is a close planted corner beside a landscaped lake. Use this name throughout the chapter, menu and picture dialog. The completed scene must read as a coherent place before its eight mathematical contributions are considered. Its design decisions are also recorded in the repository's [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).

## Plan and acceptance

Observe real photographs first: plant nodes, leaf shape and venation, flower attachment, fruit suspension, bamboo support, bank material, water perspective and night lighting. Plan the whole composition and physical connections, then place the craftable curves. Review complete, growing and empty-outline states at desktop, 390px and 320px portrait, and short landscape. GPT-6-Astra Max reviewers independently challenge botanical structure, landscape and whole-picture quality; the main agent integrates the corrections and inspects their rendered result.

The cucumber is the foreground character; a warm moon and small yellow flower balance it across cooler water. Broad calm water, grouped tree canopies and one continuous far shore establish the lake; a short, quiet path behind the foreground planting establishes a tended garden. Quiet central space keeps its depth readable. There is no distant farmhouse. The vine and bamboo share a planted bed; roots enter soil, stalks connect to real vine nodes, fruit hangs below its attachment, and loose ties support the vine without crossing fruit. Broad, shallow-lobed cucumber leaves have roughly balanced heart/triangular shapes, teeth and palmate veins. The separate reed has a narrow pointed leaf. The flower has a short pedicel, green calyx and five petals; its node remains visible outside the blossom.

The bank fill and water boundary use the same earned kernel curve. Soil shading is clipped to that silhouette, with no permanent edge stroke. Grass and shoreline-stone ground contacts derive from the sampled shore, so they remain on land when the bank changes. Small contact shadows and an irregular solid-earth patch give the bank depth. The bamboo paints in front of the bank, with only its planted tip covered by soil, so it shares the vine’s ground plane. Distant tree roots sit beside the path. Water and ripples paint behind the bank. The crafted ripple arc generates its reflected and scaled echoes, with open side gaps to avoid closed lozenges. They share one shallow perspective and center. The moon uses circular arcs through the kernel’s sampled extrema for its fill, ghost and selection contour. Moonlight forms irregular, asymmetric broken glints below it, without repeated ladder-like rows. Keep light direction consistent across leaf, fruit, soil and stone.

Acceptance considers beauty and legibility of the complete picture, not a checklist of isolated principles. Reviewers found and prompted corrections to earlier detached branches, oversized leaves, an ambiguous flower base, a heavy ripple badge, water painted in front of land, roots entering a stone, a fruit touching the bank and a tie crossing the young fruit. A subsequent user review caught the conflicting bank overlay, which was removed. Visual review is evidence of inspection, not a claim of participant satisfaction.

## Shared artwork and branding

`web/public/cucumber.svg` is the single canonical drawing. Its rounded, gently bowed body, ridges and bumps share a longitudinal axis. The garden's main fruit and smaller developing fruit reuse it; the smaller fruit omits the face. The wordmark retains the full body and removes only the separately marked stem, then adds an aligned dot. Its orientation is judged against the surrounding n/r stems. The curved upper half needs an optical 17° overall lean; merely matching the endpoint chord to the font's stem angle looked too upright. The full body fits the actual font's x-height and baseline with rounded overshoot, anchored to the text baseline across browsers. The dot follows the body axis with a .083em clear gap, optically closer than the font's i because of the rounded crown. Both scale with the font on compact screens.
