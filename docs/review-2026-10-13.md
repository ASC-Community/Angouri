## Findings and changes

### Exact mathematics and visible geometry

The highest-priority mathematical finding is an **exact false hit** caused by approximate simplification before equality proof. A legal source-64 challenge with `D Q S Q Q Q Q Q S A`, crop `[1/3, 5/12]`, and target height `1` at `x=1/3` can round a strictly greater value to `1`. Related probes found imported near-equal constants and sine-landmark normalization vulnerable to the same loss of exactness. Keep the unrounded expression through import, pointwise propagation and landmark recognition; approximate structural equality cannot prove a hit. Bounded reproductions establish these defects, not correctness over the entire expression language.

Two real-locus defects were also corrected: source 48 with `Q N` followed by 32 halves fabricated a zero path from a small negative radicand; source 48 with `S F` joined two open zero intervals across a point where the real heights are instead `±1`. The integrated kernel establishes real-domain membership before numerical cleanup and preserves exact endpoint ownership when joining paths.

A later independent review also exposed loss of an isolated real point near a Crop boundary and admission of an outside point when exact bounds round to the same double. The integrated fix compares rational and algebraic root membership exactly before drawing deduplication; four sub-double-precision inclusion/exclusion cases are permanent regressions. The resulting full native suite passes 1,349,953 assertions.

Presentation changes in [geometry.ts](../web/src/geometry.ts) and [views.ts](../web/src/views.ts) render isolated closed points, retain endpoint markers in Both heights/Crop, and separate real heights from squared-height stages. The integrated kernel contract supplies exact and numeric presentation fields for symbolic target squares, and both pre-crop and restricted geometry for an implicit terminal Crop. ### Persistence, imports and recovery

The review found destructive stale-tab writes to both saved recipes and completions, a save/restore race past the active-library limit, and reset success reported despite storage failure. [persistence.ts](../web/src/persistence.ts) now gives library entries stable IDs and performs coordinated read/modify/write transactions against current durable data. IndexedDB is the primary authority; existing local-storage bytes migrate once and later local-storage writes are only a best-effort compatibility copy. Capacity is checked at commit time; recovery copies and unknown/legacy entries are preserved. Permanent removal from Recently deleted requires an explicit named confirmation. Completion additions merge within an epoch; reset and full progress-file replacement start a new epoch so stale tabs cannot resurrect old wins. Failed replacement preserves the current workspace and record.

Links previously could replace the global completion record, bypass unsaved-work protection, or cause an invalid/stale startup request to overwrite a valid saved workspace. [main.ts](../web/src/main.ts) now validates first, settles pending edits before the navigation guard, restricts encoded links to creations/challenges, and reserves completion replacement for explicit progress-file import or reset. Navigation ownership is checked across storage waits as well as kernel awaits. Invalid input has its own recovery message. Export progress states its scope: current workspace and completions, with the named library retained on the device.

Concurrent worker failures previously started competing recovery epochs, and the last timeout could leave a worker running. [engine.ts](../web/src/engine.ts) shares one replacement per failed epoch and retires workers, handlers, timers and waiters on every terminal failure. Request deadlines start at dispatch, and interactive work precedes queued Notes/Garden work; waiting jobs do not consume a retry budget before the worker receives them. Obsolete Crop-preview errors cannot cancel a newer gesture. Regression entry points: [persistence units](../tests/persistence.test.mjs), [cross-tab scenarios](../tests/browser/persistence-concurrency.spec.mjs), [import navigation](../tests/browser/import-navigation.spec.mjs), [engine units](../tests/engine.test.mjs), and [Crop units](../tests/crop-editor.test.mjs).
