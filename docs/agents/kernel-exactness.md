## Implementation and verification

Integration is the anchored signed area `I(h)(x) = integral from 0 to x of h(u) du`. Its result starts at zero; a later lift chooses a different starting height. There is no arbitrary integration constant in this game block. The kernel evaluates the antiderivative and subtracts its value at zero. Flow shades the input curve up to the slider: solid positive area adds, hatched negative area subtracts. Show the kernel's next-stage value as the accumulated total. Reuse the shared throw clock and restore the inspected position afterward; never add a frontend integrator. Derivative and integral steps need their incoming tangent/area relationship, rather than a pointwise before-to-after height comparison.

## Loops and the playful ending

Flight follows complete curves in the locus. Join two height branches that form a simple closed loop or meet at one shared endpoint without interior intersections; an open joined curve launches from an outer endpoint, passes through the shared tip, then reaches the other outer endpoint. At crossings, continue smoothly through zero using an exactly proved signed square factor times the square root of the remaining radicand, rather than taking the principal root's absolute-value cusp. Keep the remainder under one radical so individually negative factors can still have a real product. AngouriMath's bounded exact polynomial factoriser may expose factors hidden by display simplification; verify the assembled square by exact equality and cache the proof with the solver. Equation still displays the original solved branches and real conditions. The original right side owns the real domain, including isolated zeros, and samples never prove algebraic identities. Establish real-domain membership before numerical drawing cleanup: a small negative radicand must not become an invented zero-height path or enable Throw. Natural radical tips can join these smooth crossing branches into one connected stroke: a figure-eight uses one cucumber and passes straight through its middle twice. Do not join open crossing curves merely because clipping ends at a shared square-factor zero. Pair disconnected nonrounded real regions separately. Floor and Ceiling keep one traveller per height solution and leave their jumps unconnected. Keep playback endpoints and zero anchors exact when resampling. Never start one traveller in the middle and double back to fill an unvisited tail. The kernel supplies grouping, playback ranges and breaks; all travellers and target confirmations share the reward clock. Tangent, pullback and release agree for each launcher. Reserve pullback space at both sides of relation plots because an open joined curve may launch from the right endpoint.

Ordered equations are built as AngouriMath expression trees and rendered with Latexize so precedence supplies necessary parentheses. Definite integration uses a scoped dummy variable under its integral sign; the evaluated output is expressed in x. Repeated integrals must not capture the enclosing x. Build bound derivative/integral nodes directly: substituting an upper-limit placeholder through a deep integral tree can grow exponentially in AngouriMath 2.5. Keep the 32-integral creation regression; upstream substitution performance is tracked in ASC-Community/AngouriMath#1808. Displaying a bound u is legitimate; leaking it into the evaluated equation is not.

Picture targets may have exact algebraic heights. Use kernel targetNumber and targetLatex for placement and display; never parse radicals with the rational-only frontend helper. Keep exact expressions authoritative and decimal values presentational. Recognize only the known pre-landmark picture target lists when upgrading authored saves; preserve legacy shared-challenge heights and reject arbitrary rule changes.

## Curve transitions and player language

Before simplifying exact sine checkpoint differences, try certified rational enclosures of the unexpanded substituted expression. Bound pi with Machin's identity and alternating-series remainders, reduce phases with outward interval arithmetic, and enclose sine/cosine with Taylor remainder and argument-error bounds. Recognize power exponents through exact rational arithmetic, never tolerance-based simplification. Only disjoint intervals prove a miss; overlap or an unsupported expression must fall back to symbolic equality and can never award a hit. Constant-value equality disables numeric downcasting so a tiny nonzero difference cannot round to zero. Restore exact rational arithmetic and known sine/cosine landmark and half-angle identities before expansion and symbolic cancellation; approximate decimals must never supply proof integers. Construct Integer/Rational nodes directly in this scope: parsing with downcasting disabled can cache approximate numeral nodes that contaminate later requests. Preserve the general symbolic path for expressions with free variables. Keep exact values and LaTeX from the kernel, apply the same proof path to the height gap, and retain bounded work and exact target rules.
