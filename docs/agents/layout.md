# Layout

Mandatory contract from the [Angouri working guidance](../../AGENTS.md) whenever a change affects dialog chrome, zoom and reflow, inspection scroll, label space, the viewport or scrolling. Code paths are repository-relative. Interactive changes also require [focus-keyboard](focus-keyboard.md); view content is in [views](views.md).

## Interaction language

Dialog Back/Close controls sit outside the scrollable body. Notes has one viewport-bounded size across lesson buttons; its navigation remains above a separate reading scroller, and each selected lesson begins at the top. Full curve stays outside the centered view selector on the same toolbar row; compact layouts show its named icon with the accessible Full curve label. Content that fits centers vertically within its own content area, never relative to the page. Preserve recipe-body and inspection scroll through edits/history/view changes; explicitly opening another construction resets inspection. Do not resize the document during a render to protect scrolling. Discard uses the same danger-red trash icon as other destructive recipe actions.

## Visual consistency

Browser zoom enlarges controls and text and causes reflow. Flight keeps KaTeX labels in an HTML overlay projected through the SVG screen coordinate transform. Do not put positioned KaTeX inside Flight foreignObject labels: Safari may paint their contents at the SVG origin. Flight fits a fixed-aspect diagram with stable mathematical bounds; resizing must not stretch the curve. Keep its KaTeX labels readable in CSS pixels while fitting the diagram. Flow cards keep useful dimensions, share aligned plot baselines and become a vertical scrollable chain when narrow; extra space should not inflate their contents. The game document never scrolls: allocate the viewport to its heading, shared recipe and remaining central content area. All three views retain the same outer frame. Use local inspection and recipe scrollers at cramped sizes, preserving readable controls and reachable content. Flow uses taller, narrower cards when its actual chain area has enough height, and centers a fitting chain vertically. Large content scrolls from its start instead of being centered into an inaccessible negative overflow.

Preserve local recipe and view inspection scroll while replacing their content. The game document stays fixed; do not add a temporary body minimum height or issue window scroll calls on accepted edits. Revealing a placed or moved block adjusts only its horizontal recipe containers. Check 8.7 in Flight, Equation and Flow through placement, drag cancellation, return and Undo/Redo, including intermediate frames. Batch dense-label geometry reads before writes, and rebuild the puzzle menu only when its source, mode or completion record changes.

Reserve space for graph labels within the scene, above the recipe tray. Check the axis labels themselves at narrow and short viewport sizes, not only the outer SVG dimensions. The loaded cucumber sits behind the fork, in the pulled-back pouch, with the stretched bands behind it. After release, the empty pouch hangs horizontally between the fork tips; both bands remain attached to its edges throughout the motion.

## Viewport and scrolling

The game fills the available viewport without document overflow. Flight, Equation and Flow share one central frame, with the recipe header and launch action outside the tray-body scroller. Equation and Flow keep their content inside that frame and scroll locally; compact Flow scrolls its vertical chain within the remaining content area. Preserve a usable diagram and complete inspectable cards through local scrolling rather than growing the document. Validation and confirmation must not resize the frame or move Throw/Next.

Treat scrolling as a deliberate content decision. Reference formulas, block labels, view tabs and dialog headers fit whole. A long recipe scrolls horizontally; Flow scrolls across desktop or down narrow panels. A large final expression or value table may scroll within its own section; a tall menu/reference scrolls vertically with its Back header retained. Compact Create uses a three-by-three palette for nine reusable operations and a usable minimum board height. Validation changes only existing marks and must not change these scroll extents.

Circle Flow target choices may wrap at wider widths. At compact widths, keep them on one horizontally scrolling row so their labels cannot consume the diagram area; every named target remains reachable. Its reading area scrolls locally within the shared frame.

Dense Flight retains a usable complete diagram inside its own scroller when the shared central frame is too short for the plot and callouts. Keep its SVG out of intrinsic flex sizing and give it the scene's allocated height minus the callout rows explicitly; do not rely on a chain of percentage heights through auto-sized flex containers. In landscape, let the canvas shrink to the width left by the recipe. Measure the settled HTML/SVG boxes before taking the label projection matrix. Check the actual diagram and plot bounds, not only the scene box, on initial load and while the same dense puzzle moves between portrait and landscape; sources 54 and 80 cover both station and direct-choice layouts.

The garden picture fits the actual space remaining between its heading and footer, including wrapped text. Its board must never enlarge that grid row or overlap the status and Done control. Use the wide landscape composition for short, wide windows; retain a usable minimum picture and an intentional dialog-shell scroller when height is exhausted. That dialog scroller does not permit the underlying game document to grow.

Ordinary controls keep readable sizes, and long formulas or comparisons can scroll in both necessary directions inside their section. At compact widths, chapter titles, progress and disclosure arrows fit inside their summary; saved-recipe names/context take a full row above Open/Rename. Test actual child bounds and intermediate frames as well as scrollWidth. Check Chromium, Firefox and WebKit at responsive boundaries, narrow portrait, short landscape and zoom-equivalent CSS viewports; browser-engine automation does not establish native Safari or physical-device behavior.
