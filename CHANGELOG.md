# Version log

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

The first playable Angouri cucumber puzzle.

- Fifty-six puzzles in eleven chapters: Height, Reflection, Squaring, Repeated squaring, Slopes, Accumulation, Circle geometry, Two heights, Waves, Steps and Together. Every source is a distinct step and completion. Eleven earlier shape puzzles remain in an optional collection, for 67 playable sources in total.
- The final challenge combines slope, phase, steps, signed accumulation, centering, powers and both branches of an equation. Its lead-up builds those connections through smaller mixed puzzles.
- Waves connect a circle's height to phase, period and amplitude. Steps distinguish floor and ceiling, preserve exact open/closed boundaries and teach piecewise accumulation.
- Circle recipe puzzles reuse familiar roofs as the right side of a squared-height equation. AngouriMath solves for both real heights; playback follows joined branches smoothly through zero. The opening frame keeps required targets readable, while explicit Fit includes the full unfinished curve. Circle target verdicts update during dragging without rebuilding or remeasuring the diagram.
- A complete circle chapter explores two heights at one position, a shared centre and radius, diameters and perpendicular bisectors. Move the centre or drag the slingshot to resize the circle; tap destinations, arrow keys and exact fraction fields also work. AngouriMath validates squared distances; the cucumber travels the whole loop.
- Fixed calculus stations let you shape both their input and output within the familiar viewport and horizontal recipe. Their raised, mounted surfaces distinguish them from movable blocks; only usable sides show slots. Flow places each slope sketch or signed-area meter before its calculus output plot—on the left, or above it on narrow screens—tied to the same slider and reward clock.
- Repeated squaring explores fourth, sixth and eighth powers in sequence. Its final puzzle follows the provided-bowl summit by requiring the player to construct that bowl first.
- Flight's KaTeX labels use an HTML overlay to keep their positions consistent in Safari. Block stacks have close, aligned edges without connector nubs; reusable stacks fade and refill from below.
- Keyboard navigation retains focus after edits and Undo, and view-tab arrows follow the focused tab.
- Dashed square placeholders represent the incoming expression in every operation block. Completed equations include their left side; Halve, Add one, Negate and Square use general names.
- A minimal first puzzle starts with a large centred wordmark, curve and two block choices. Puzzle 1.2 adds context and a third choice; 1.3 introduces ordering, the editor, Shape notes and the three views. Later single-slot lessons use direct choices while retaining their heading, notes and views. Clicking a selected choice again clears it, with Undo/Redo available.
- Nine reusable operations include sine, floor, ceiling, differentiation and signed accumulated area from zero, with exact values supplied by the kernel.
- The combined final garden opens an honest puzzle record, an AngouriMath credit, and routes to Create or revisit puzzles. Unplayed sources are never marked complete.
- Notes provide reusable principles and independent examples without current-puzzle targets or solution plans. The current concept comes first; earlier numbered lessons remain directly visible, while future concepts stay hidden on early puzzles and replays. Create has the complete reference.
- Hints hold puzzle-specific clues and planning sketches. Notes and view shortcuts come before the optional Another hint and Show a sketch disclosures. Both reference and hint diagrams share fixed scales and exact kernel readings without editing the player's construction.
- The **Hints** light bulb gently glows yellow after repeated rearrangements without more target matches, continuing until opened or solved and restarting on each further rearrangement. Filling slots and throwing do not count. Notes and the suggested view come before the explicit Another hint button, whose triangle indicates its expanded state.
- Equation and Flow references in Notes use their outlined icon buttons and open the corresponding view. Earlier Function links and saves retain their identifiers.
- Flight, Equation and Flow show the same construction, with immediate target feedback and a shared throwing animation. Flow shades positive and negative input area for integration steps and explains squared distances for circles.
- Equation shows the ordered construction above its simplified form, with kernel-rendered symbolic values. Solved heights and their real conditions have separate readable lines. Exact fractions have decimal reading aids, using equals for exact expansions, approximation signs for rounded values, and powers of ten for decimal exponents.
- From puzzle 1.3 through the polynomial chapters, Equation compares a signed height gap and Flow marks its size and direction at each step. Height-at-position notation refers to the same highest/lowest target positions throughout edits. Circle equations instead compare distances from their centre.
- Drag, tap or use the keyboard to build recipes, move empty slots and return blocks to their stacks. Undo and Redo keep experimentation easy.
- Returning a block shows its receiving outline above the remaining stack, preserving visible stock counts and stable layout. Block selection keeps Flight on the complete recipe; Flow provides the stage comparisons.
- Create your own curves with reusable blocks, save recipes and share challenges. Save or discard before switching away from unsaved work; resetting progress keeps saved recipes and settings.
- Saved recipes show their puzzle/Create context and saved date and time beneath the name. Rename edits the name in place without changing the recipe or its date; Enter saves and Escape cancels.
- Skip animation, community links and an About page with Angouri’s open-source projects.
