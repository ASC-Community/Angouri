# Version log

## 0.1.0 — Unreleased

The first playable Angouri cucumber puzzle.

- Twenty-eight puzzles in six chapters: Height, Reflection, Squaring, Flatter tops, Slopes and Area. Each chapter now ends with a larger planning puzzle.
- A minimal first puzzle starts with the logo, curve and two block choices. Puzzle 1.2 adds context and a third choice; 1.3 introduces ordering, the editor, Shape notes and the three views.
- Six reusable operations include differentiation and signed accumulated area from zero, with exact values supplied by the kernel.
- The final flight opens a chapter record, an AngouriMath credit, and routes to Create or revisit puzzles.
- Shape notes connect familiar curves into larger constructions, revealing ideas as each puzzle introduces them. Earlier lessons, including 1.5’s gap and fractional-lift guidance, remain available in later chapters and on replay.
- Shape notes gently pulse after repeated rearrangements without more target matches, continuing until opened or solved and restarting on each further rearrangement. Filling slots and throwing do not count.
- Function and Flow references in Shape notes use their outlined icon buttons and open the corresponding view.
- Flight, Function and Flow show the same recipe, with immediate target feedback and a shared throwing animation. Flow shades positive and negative input area for integration steps.
- Function pairs exact fractions with readable decimals, using equals for exact expansions and approximation signs for rounded values.
- From puzzle 1.3 through the final chapter, Function compares a signed height gap and Flow marks its size and direction at each step. Both use the positions of the lowest and highest required targets, held fixed while editing; puzzle 3.5 compares the heights at 1 and 4.
- Drag, tap or use the keyboard to build recipes, move empty slots and return blocks to their stacks. Undo and Redo keep experimentation easy.
- Returning a block shows its receiving outline above the remaining stack, preserving visible stock counts and stable layout. Block selection keeps Flight on the complete recipe; Flow provides the stage comparisons.
- Create your own curves with reusable blocks, save recipes and share challenges. Save or discard before switching away from unsaved work; resetting progress keeps saved recipes and settings.
- Saved recipes show their puzzle/Create context and saved date and time beneath the name.
- Skip animation, community links and an About page with Angouri’s open-source projects.
